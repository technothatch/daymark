# Privacy

Daymark is local-first by design.

- Tasks, notes, mood history, intention memory, and statistics stay in browser `localStorage`.
- Mood and timer preferences use first-party `SameSite=Lax` cookies so they can return after a refresh.
- No data is sent to a Daymark server.
- No analytics, tracking pixels, account system, or advertising SDK is included.
- Browser focus notifications are opt-in and controlled by the browser.
- Use **Export** to create a local JSON backup.
- Use **Clear local data** to remove the saved Daymark state from the current browser.

The static SEO pages do not require an account and do not collect form submissions.
