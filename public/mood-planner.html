The mood choice is not a score and does not diagnose anything. It is a small planning input: a way to choose an appropriate session, cue, and amount of effort for today.

Private by design

Is this a mood tracker?

It is a lightweight daily check-in, not a medical or mental-health assessment.

Does the mood change the app?

Yes. It changes the color atmosphere, daily cue, and recommended focus length.

Is my mood uploaded?

No. The selection is stored locally in your browser with the rest of your Daymark data.

Can I change it later?

Yes. The mood selector stays at the top of the main app and can be changed anytime.
