A calmer daily planner

Plan the day without turning it into a second job.

Daymark is a free daily planner for choosing a few meaningful intentions, working in focused stretches, and clearing your head. It works in your browser with no account and keeps your notes on your device.

Start planning for free

Choose what matters

Keep a short list of concrete intentions instead of an endless backlog. Check them off, remove them, or add your own in seconds.

Make time visible

Use a five, twenty-five, or fifty-minute focus session and see your completed intentions and focus minutes add up.

Keep it private

Your daily plan, mood, and notes stay in local browser storage. There is no sign-in, server, or tracking account.

Why Daymark

A simple planner for real days.

The best daily plan is small enough to use when you are busy. Daymark combines a task shortlist, focus timer, private note, mood check-in, export, and local statistics in one quiet workspace.

Questions

Is this daily planner free?

Yes. Daymark is a free browser tool with no account required.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The planner is responsive and works in current mobile and desktop browsers.

Can I save or export my plan?

Yes. Your current Daymark data can be exported as a JSON file from the app.

Do you store my tasks?

No. Tasks and notes stay locally in the browser on the device you use.
