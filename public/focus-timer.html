A focus timer with less noise

Twenty-five minutes can change the shape of a day.

Daymark is a free online focus timer for studying, writing, coding, reading, and getting one important thing moving. Choose the length that fits your energy, start, and let the browser mark the finish.

Start a focus session

Choose your pace

Use five minutes for a reset, twenty-five minutes for a classic focus block, or fifty minutes for deeper work.

Know when it ends

Enable an optional browser notification for completed sessions. The app also shows an in-page completion message.

No login required

Your timer runs locally in the browser. No account, subscription, server, or tracking is needed.

More than a timer

Focus with a plan, not a countdown alone.

Daymark pairs the timer with a small intention list and a private unload note, so you can name the next action before you start and leave the mental clutter somewhere when you finish.

Good for

Study sessions

Choose a short or standard session, then return to the material with a clear stopping point.

Deep work

Use a longer session for writing, coding, design, research, or any work that benefits from fewer switches.

Small resets

A five-minute session is enough to begin a task, clear friction, or make the next step visible.

Private focus

Daymark does not send your timer history to a service. Your focus minutes remain on your device.
